Good to know

The AI model is a free, no-account service run by Pollinations (terms, privacy). It is slow on purpose: its documentation allows one free request at a time, at least 30 seconds apart per IP address. It is best effort, so it can fail or change. Small models make mistakes, and that is part of the point. Your text is sent to Pollinations and the model providers it uses, so don't type anything private. Pollinations says its service is for people aged 16 and over. Needs a recent browser (Safari 12 or newer).
