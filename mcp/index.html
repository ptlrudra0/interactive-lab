Read this first

The MCP server and client are both real (the official MCP SDK) but run inside this page and talk through the SDK's in-memory link, not over the network. The AI model is a separate free service, Pollinations, called straight from your browser with no account. Its documentation says free requests are limited per IP address to one at a time, at least 30 seconds apart, so each answer is slow and a tools question needs two or more model calls. It is best effort and can fail. Small models get things wrong; that is part of what you are seeing. Your question and any tool results are sent to Pollinations and its model providers, so do not type anything private. Pollinations says its service is for people aged 16 and over.
